# DON'T LET IT IN

A 30-second horror game over your live selfie.

Shadow creatures creep in from the edges of the screen, straight for your face. Tap them to banish them in a burst of smoke before they reach you. Three lives, a quiet heartbeat, monster growls — and if you leave the frame, the game pauses and waits for you.

## Play it

**GitHub Pages:** https://bubblycouchsurfer.github.io/dont-let-it-in/

Or open `index.html` in any modern browser. Camera stays on your device — nothing is recorded or uploaded. No camera? The game runs in shadow mode too.

## How to play

- **Tap** a creature to banish it: +10 points (combo x2 after 3 in a row, x3 after 6)
- A creature that reaches your face costs **1 of 3 hearts**
- Survive the full **30 seconds**
- Leaving view pauses the game ("WHERE DID YOU GO?"); coming back resumes it
